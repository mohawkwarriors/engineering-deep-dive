## Manufacturing & Thermal Transfer Sequence

<ProcessFlow>
  <ProcessStep step="01" title="Capillary Tube Pre-Forming" parameters="AISI 304 • OD 4.0mm / ID 2.4mm">
    Annealed stainless conduit is bent on a CNC rotary-draw mandrel with internal mandrel support to limit ovality under 4%.
  </ProcessStep>
  <ProcessStep step="02" title="Cavity Placement & Insert Pre-Heat" parameters="280°C pre-soak">
    Formed conduit and tubular calrod heating elements are robotically seated into tool steel dies, minimizing chill-pin flash.
  </ProcessStep>
  <ProcessStep step="03" title="High-Pressure Aluminum Squeeze" parameters="A380 Alloy • 65 MPa dwell">
    Semi-solid liquid aluminum is injected under high pressure to metallurgically bed the conduit, eliminating insulating micro-voids.
  </ProcessStep>
  <ProcessStep step="04" title="Final Calibration Hydrostatic Burst Check" parameters="18 Bar • 30s dwell">
    Chamber is pressurized with DI water to calibrate internal wall clearance and confirm zero leakage along the swaged interface.
  </ProcessStep>
</ProcessFlow>
