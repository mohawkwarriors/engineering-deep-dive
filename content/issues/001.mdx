## 1. The Two-Pronged Hardware Strategy

Rather than manufacturing low-margin, high-liability residential fixtures in-house, Apple split its smart home ecosystem at the drywall boundary. Apple retains complete ownership of the silicon and display layer—the HomeView 7-inch smart display, Apple TV 4K, and HomePod mini—while partnering with LG Electronics and Schneider Electric to manufacture the physical sensors, actuators, and high-voltage wall hardware.

## 2. Spatial Unlocking Kinematics & Privacy Actuation
