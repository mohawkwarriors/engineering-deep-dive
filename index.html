    Inside the Apple HomeView & Apple Home Collection: The Complete Hardware Breakdown

    Everything Apple just unveiled at its "Welcome Home" event—from the $299 Liquid Retina command hub to the co-engineered lineup of UWB locks, motorized privacy cameras, and radar thermostats built with LG Electronics and Schneider Electric.

    1. The Big Picture: What Apple Just Announced

    For the past decade, Apple’s smart home strategy relied on a fragmented "bring-your-own-accessory" model. While Apple built the brains—Apple TV and HomePod—it left security cameras, doorbells, smart locks, and thermostats entirely to third-party manufacturers, forcing users to juggle multiple apps and mismatched white plastics.

    At its October "Welcome Home" event in New York, Apple overhauled that entire model with a two-part hardware offensive: first-party command hubs (Apple HomeView, $299) and the Apple Home Collection (seven residential accessories co-developed with LG Electronics and Schneider Electric).

    2. How the JDM Partnership Worked

    Unlike standard "Works with Apple Home" accessories where manufacturers design independently, the Apple Home Collection is a true Joint Design Manufacturing (JDM) partnership. Apple's Industrial Design group co-engineered the CMF (Essence Silver, Graphite, and White), zero-app NFC commissioning, and end-to-end encrypted iCloud architecture, while LG and Schneider handled high-voltage UL compliance, mechanical lock durability, and HVAC hardware engineering.

    3. Product-by-Product Deep Dive

    Apple HomeView (Tabletop & Wall Hub) $299.00

    Available in two mechanical variants at $299: the Tabletop Model (featuring a precision stainless-steel tilt hinge mounted on a domed acoustic speaker base) and the Wall-Mounted Model which snaps onto Schneider Home Power Mounts (USB-C, AC gang-box, or PoE). Features a 0.3-nit to 500-nit dimming range and 6-user local Face ID profile switching.

    LG Smart Deadbolt Lock $349.95

    Utilizes IEEE 802.15.4z Ultra-Wideband (UWB) time-of-flight ranging and Aliro standards to verify your exterior approach vector within ±3 cm, preventing false indoor triggers while throwing the bolt open automatically.

    LG Security Camera Lineup $199.95 – $399.95

    Features the Indoor Cam's motorized privacy gimbal (physically tilting the lens into an opaque housing when occupied) and the Video Doorbell's dual-lens optical split for simultaneous visitor and package monitoring.

    LG & Schneider Smart Thermostats $199.95

    Features automated on-device wire detection, multi-room sensor fusion with HomePods and HomeView, and iPhone Adaptive Temperature.
